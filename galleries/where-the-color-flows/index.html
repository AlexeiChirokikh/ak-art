Where the Color Flows

Against a dark field, color gathers into dense bands, then breaks apart into individual droplets. The paint has already set, but the eye still follows its movement. Moving from one canvas to the next, we recognize familiar forms or discover new facets of what we already know.

The paintings are deliberately untitled. Each viewer is free to trust their own associations, give what they see a name of their own, or leave it open.
