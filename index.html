Anzhelika Korneeva was born in Siberia, Russia, where winter temperatures can fall to forty below zero, the taiga stretches to the horizon, and Lake Baikal, the deepest lake on Earth, is known for its clear water.

Work brought her to Moscow, where she became a department director at a major energy company and led the introduction of artificial intelligence and robotics at large power plants. Her days were full: time with her son and friends, interviews, conferences, and negotiations.

Then she met a man she felt she had known all her life. With each conversation, they felt more certain they had been waiting for each other. They fell in love. He lived in Canada and later in the United States, so she left her career and friends in Moscow to be with him. They settled in Florida.

In Florida, the blue lakes, lush greenery, and friendly people made her want to share the warmth she felt in her new home. She had studied drawing years earlier but had set it aside to focus on her career. She returned to art and began making textured, three-dimensional paintings that express her hope for a brighter future.

Through her work, Anzhelika hopes to remind viewers that wonderful things can happen and to share a little of the joy that inspired her to paint again.
